Our story

Northwest Missouri needed an upfitter. So we became one.

Located in Northwest Missouri, Midwest Public Safety Equipment, LLC is a full-service emergency vehicle upfitter and public safety equipment outfitter. Established in 2020, we saw the lack of this service in our region and made it our goal to provide an affordable service to individuals and public safety agencies anywhere in the United States.

We're run by first responders, so we know what a truck needs to do on a call. We offer the products consumers and agencies rely on to do their job effectively every day.

  • Emergency lighting & sirens
  • Radios & communications
  • Consoles & mounts
  • Uniforms & duty gear
  • Grant writing help
  • CPR / BLS classes
