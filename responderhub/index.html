What it does

Every hub your agency needs

Turn each hub on or off per agency. One login per employee, nothing to install.

Scheduling that fills itself

Rotations like 24/48, Kelly or your own, auto-fill by certification, shift bids, trades, call-ins and time off.

Hours you can defend

GPS-located punches checked against the schedule, approved timesheets and payroll export with 7(k) overtime rules.

Rigs, stations & narcotics

Rig checks that open work orders, preventive maintenance, supply restock and a witnessed narcotics chain of custody.

Paperwork without the paper

W-4, I-9, direct deposit and exposure reports signed in the app and encrypted at rest.

Command from the first unit

An editable ICS board, pre-plans and maps, ICS forms and personnel hours per incident.

A front door for the public

Public notices, messages and records requests, all landing in one office inbox.

Who it's for

Built for responders

Ambulance districts & EMS

Career, part-time and PRN crews across stations and units, with narcotics custody and supply tracking built in.

Fire departments

Career and combination departments: apparatus checks that create work orders, pre-plans and maps, an ICS board and training due dates.

County & multi-agency systems

Several agencies on one platform, each with its own database, logins, branding and feature set.
